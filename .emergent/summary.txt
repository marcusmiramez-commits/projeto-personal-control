It features distinct interfaces for professionals (admin) and students, with direct WhatsApp integration and PDF report generation. The goal is to automate management, replacing manual spreadsheets.

1.  **Student Management:** CRUD operations for students (name, email, phone, age, objective, contract type, photo). Integrated student creation with WhatsApp data sending option and editing.

4.  **Financial Module:** Dashboard for professionals (total received, pendencies, revenue graphs) and students (payment history, class balance, due dates). Integrated with attendance for revenue reports.
